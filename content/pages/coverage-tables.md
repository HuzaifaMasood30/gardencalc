# Coverage Reference Tables

These tables gather the coverage and depth figures behind the calculators in one place, so
you can sanity-check a number without working through the maths. Each row links back to the
tool that produced it.

## Mulch coverage

Mulch is usually sold by the cubic yard in bulk or by the 2 cubic foot bag. Spread three
inches deep, one cubic yard covers about 108 square feet.

| Depth | 2 cu ft bags per 100 sq ft | Cubic yards per 1,000 sq ft |
|---|---|---|
| 2 in | 8 | 6.2 |
| 3 in | 13 | 9.3 |
| 4 in | 17 | 12.3 |
| 6 in | 25 | 18.5 |

See the [mulch calculator](BASE/mulch-calculator/) and the [flower bed guide](BASE/how-much-mulch-do-i-need-for-a-flower-bed/).

## Soil and raised beds

A raised bed is a fixed volume, so the depth sets the fill. One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet,
or about 18 bags of 1.5 cubic feet.

| Bed size | 6 in deep | 12 in deep | 18 in deep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 x 4 ft | 8 cu ft | 16 cu ft | 24 cu ft |
| 4 x 8 ft | 16 cu ft | 32 cu ft | 48 cu ft |
| 4 x 12 ft | 24 cu ft | 48 cu ft | 72 cu ft |

See the [raised bed soil calculator](BASE/raised-bed-soil-calculator/) and the
[topsoil calculator](BASE/topsoil-calculator/).

## Gravel and sand

Gravel and sand are ordered by the cubic yard or the ton. A cubic yard weighs roughly 1.4
tons, though this varies with the type and moisture.

| Depth | Cubic yards per 100 sq ft | Approx. tons per 100 sq ft |
|---|---|---|
| 2 in | 0.62 | 0.87 |
| 3 in | 0.93 | 1.3 |
| 4 in | 1.23 | 1.72 |

See the [gravel calculator](BASE/gravel-calculator/) and the [sand calculator](BASE/sand-calculator/).

## Concrete

Concrete is ordered by the cubic yard, and bags are sold by weight. An 80 lb bag yields
about 0.6 cubic feet; a 60 lb bag about 0.45 cubic feet.

| Slab size | 4 in thick | 80 lb bags | 60 lb bags |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 x 4 ft | 0.2 cu yd | 9 | 12 |
| 8 x 8 ft | 0.79 cu yd | 36 | 48 |
| 10 x 10 ft | 1.23 cu yd | 56 | 75 |
| 20 x 20 ft | 4.94 cu yd | 223 | 297 |

See the [concrete calculator](BASE/concrete-calculator/) and the
[10x10 slab guide](BASE/how-many-bags-of-concrete-do-i-need-for-a-10x10-slab/).

## Paint

Paint coverage is quoted per litre or per gallon, usually for one coat. Add a second coat
for a colour change and a little more for a porous surface.

| Room | Paintable area | Gallons, 2 coats |
|---|---|---|
| 10 x 10 ft | 276 sq ft | 1.6 |
| 12 x 12 ft | 320 sq ft | 1.8 |
| 15 x 15 ft | 380 sq ft | 2.2 |
| 20 x 15 ft | 460 sq ft | 2.6 |

See the [paint calculator](BASE/paint-calculator/).

## Tiles

Tile counts depend on the tile size. Divide the floor area by the area of one tile, then add
10% for a straight layout or 15% for a diagonal one.

| Tile size | Tiles per sq ft | Tiles per 100 sq ft |
|---|---|---|
| 6 x 6 in | 4 | 400 |
| 12 x 12 in | 1 | 100 |
| 18 x 18 in | 0.44 | 44 |
| 24 x 24 in | 0.25 | 25 |

See the [tile calculator](BASE/tile-calculator/).

## Grass seed and fertiliser

Grass seed is sold by weight. A new lawn takes about 4.5 lb per 1,000 square feet, and
overseeding about half that. Fertiliser is worked out from the nitrogen share on the bag.

| Area | New lawn seed | Overseeding | 3 lb bags |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 sq ft | 4.5 lb | 2 lb | 2 |
| 5,000 sq ft | 22.5 lb | 10 lb | 8 |
| 10,000 sq ft | 45 lb | 20 lb | 15 |
| 1 acre | 196 lb | 87 lb | 66 |

See the [grass seed calculator](BASE/grass-seed-calculator/) and the
[fertilizer calculator](BASE/fertilizer-calculator/).

## A note on these figures

The coverage rates here are the ones our calculators use, drawn from supplier guidance and
standard practice. Product packaging can differ, so always check the bag or the delivery
note. If a figure looks out of step with what you have been told, tell us on the
[contact page](BASE/contact/) and we will check the method.
