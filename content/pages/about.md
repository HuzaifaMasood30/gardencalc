# About

{{SITE_NAME}} is a free collection of home and garden calculators and practical how-to
guides. Each tool answers a specific, everyday question: how much mulch a bed needs, how
much soil fills a raised bed, how many bags of concrete a slab requires, and dozens of
similar problems.

## How our calculators work

Every calculator uses the standard formulas and coverage rates that suppliers publish. The
tool shows the working, not just the answer, so you can check it against the product you
are buying. Each article also includes a worked example and a table of results for common
job sizes, so you can sanity-check your own numbers.

## Why we built this

Most "how much do I need" pages either bury the answer under advertising or give a figure
with no explanation. We wanted the opposite: a fast page, the formula in plain English, and
the number you actually came for.

## How we keep it accurate

The formulas are fixed in code and reviewed against supplier guidance. If a rate changes,
the calculator is updated and the page's "last updated" date changes with it. If you spot
an error, please tell us on the [contact page](/contact/).

## Our editorial approach

We do not invent statistics or cite studies that do not exist. Where a number is an
industry rule of thumb rather than an exact figure, we say so. Where results depend on your
supplier, we point you to check their stated coverage.
